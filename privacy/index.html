Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Status Bar Studio is designed to help you edit the status-bar area of screenshots on your iPhone. We built it around a simple principle: your images should stay with you.

Images are processed on your device

Photos and screenshots you choose to edit, and images you take with the camera when you actively choose to do so, are processed locally on your iPhone. Status Bar Studio does not collect, upload, or transmit those images.

Permissions

Status Bar Studio may request access to your Photos library or Camera. These permissions are used only when you actively choose an image from Photos or take a photo within the app. You can manage permissions at any time in your iPhone settings.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Status Bar Studio does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s own terms and privacy practices apply to App Store transactions.

Information we collect

Status Bar Studio does not require an account and does not need a network connection for its editing features. We do not collect your photos or screenshots, and this website does not include forms, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Questions

This policy is intentionally limited to the product’s current behavior. If you have a question, please use the support option provided with the app or its App Store listing.
